# The emails

Last verified: 2026-11-14

Read this when you write a first email or its follow-ups. Every bracket gets a real value from the ledger, the page or `facts.md`. Change the wording from email to email so twenty of them don't read as one template. Keep the facts exact and the length short. Sign with the founder's first name only.

## Wrong line

Subject: [page title] has an old [price / limit / feature] for [product]

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm [first name], founder of [product]. Your [page title] says [the wrong line, quoted]. It's actually [the right line] (since [date], if you know it), here's our [pricing / docs] page: [proof URL].
>
> Would you mind updating it? Happy to send anything else you need for the post.
>
> [First name]

## Missing from a list (Earn)

Subject: [product] for your [list title]?

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm [first name], founder of [product]. Your [list title] is one of the pages AI keeps citing when people ask [the buyer question, in plain words] and we're not on it. [Why it helps their readers: the reader their list is for who's missing an option, one sentence.]
>
> If it fits, here's an entry in the same format as the others, for [placement: the section, next to which tool]:
>
> "[proposed mention: two sentences in their format, built from Mention in facts.md, with one number]"
>
> Happy to send [what's listed under Offer in facts.md].
>
> [First name]

Only offer what is listed under "Offer" in `facts.md`. Nothing listed: drop that sentence. Never offer money, a link swap or a review. Leave out the line about AI citing the page if the owner's site is clearly not about marketing or SEO, it reads like jargon there.

## Out of date or a one-liner (Earn, refresh)

Subject: Quick update for [product] on your [list title]

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for including [product] in your [list title]. The entry still says [what's out of date: old name, old plan, one line with no detail]. Here's an updated version in the same format, if you want it:
>
> "[proposed mention]"
>
> [First name]

## A competitor's page

Same as the wrong-line email, to their founder, friendly and plain. No threat, no "our lawyers", no "as you surely know". One extra line is fine:

> I'm sure it's just an old number, pricing pages move fast.

## Reply to a paid offer (only after the founder decides)

Yes:
> Thanks [Name], that works for us. What do you need from me to get it live?

Counter at the ceiling:
> Thanks [Name]. [Price] is more than we spend on a single placement. We could do [ceiling] for [a year / the next update]. Does that work?

No:
> Thanks for getting back to me, [Name]. We'll pass on a paid spot for now. If you update the post anyway, the right numbers are here: [proof URL].

## Follow-ups (written with the first email, sent as replies in the same thread)

Day 7:
> Hi [Name], just bumping this in case it got buried. [The one fix, in five words.]

Day 14:
> Hi [Name], one more try on this. Readers comparing [category] tools still see [the wrong line / no mention of us]. The right info is here: [proof URL].

Day 28, closes the loop (wrong line):
> Hi [Name], I'll leave it here. If you ever update the post, the right numbers are on our [pricing] page: [proof URL]. Thanks either way.

Day 28, closes the loop (missing from a list):
> Hi [Name], I'll leave it here. If you refresh the list one day and want a [category] tool for [who the product is for], we'd love a look. Thanks either way.

After the last one, the page is quiet for 90 days.
