# From lost prompt to action

Last verified: 2026-10-21

Read this when you map each lost prompt or lost AI Overview query to its one action (rule 6 in SKILL.md). Pick the action from its most cited source type. Chat prompts and Google queries share one pool and one cap of [5] actions.

- Listicle or comparison: publish your own "best [category] for [niche]" list with real alternatives on it and you on it too, around ten entries, refreshed quarterly. "Best" lists are the most cited page type in ChatGPT.
- Tracked competitor or other vendor: an honest vs page for that competitor, in a neutral voice a buyer would trust. Sites with more vs pages get more AI referral traffic.
- Review site or app store: complete the G2 profile, list it in every category you credibly fit, make its pricing match your site and ask a few customers a month "what do you use us for?" right after a real win. Review count alone explains under 1% of citations, so the push is for fresh reviews that name a use case.
- YouTube: one use-case page plus one 5 to 15 minute how-to video for that buyer question, with the answer in the first 30 seconds. YouTube carries Google's AI answers far more than ChatGPT, so rank this higher when the lost prompt is on Gemini or the lost query is on AI Overviews.
- Docs or help, or your site cited while you aren't named: make that page readable for bots. Server-rendered content, a visible "starts at" price, robots.txt letting OAI-SearchBot in. Most AI crawlers don't run JavaScript.
- Other (a publisher or a stats roundup, once you've sorted it): original data from your own product, on one stable page you update in place. Start it only after the other four are live, since the first edition takes weeks.
- Reddit, forums, other people's lists, creators: earned work. Mark it SECOND PILE. It needs someone else to say yes, so it runs steadily in the background after the control work.
